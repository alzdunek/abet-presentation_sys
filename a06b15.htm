ChE 341 - Chemical Process Control
UIC Course Description Analysis and design of chemical processes and control systems. Feedback and feedforward controllers. Stability, tuning, and simulation of P-I-D controllers. Introduction to the control of chemical plants and digital process control. Course Information: 3 hours. Prerequisite(s): MATH 220 and CHE 312 and CHE 313 and CHE 321.
