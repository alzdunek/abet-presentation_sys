ChE 230 - Molecular Systems in Chemical Engineering
UIC Course Description Introduction to fundamental concepts in molecular engineering and materials chemistry; properties of molecular systems and applications of macromolecules, biomacromolecules, and nanomaterials in energy, medicine, environment, and technology.

Role in the Curriculum

CHE 230 was added to the Chemical Engineering curriculum in 2022. It is a sophomore-level, chemical-engineering-specific materials course that establishes a foundation in molecular engineering and materials chemistry before students enter the junior-level core. The course introduces molecular engineering, macromolecular systems, and nanomaterials for traditional and emerging chemical engineering applications.

The three-credit course was added without increasing the program's 128-credit-hour degree requirement. Changes to the credit hours for CHEM 232, CHEM 234, and CHE 396 created the three-credit space that CHE 230 now fills.

Course Learning Objectives

Upon completing the course, students will be able to:

  1. Understand fundamental concepts used in materials chemistry and molecular systems.
  2. Classify molecular systems, nanomaterials, and materials systems.
  3. Describe basic methods used to synthesize and characterize materials systems.
  4. Explain the properties that characterize the behavior of molecular systems.
  5. Select an appropriate material for a specified application.
  6. Propose modifications to structure or microstructure to obtain desired properties.
Semester Project

Students complete a semester-long group project addressing polymer design, processing, and application. Each group prepares a case study and critically reviews a molecular-system application from the perspective of material properties and product-performance requirements.

Course Topics
  • Introduction to molecular systems
  • Molecular structure and defects
  • Diffusion and material properties derived from molecular structure
  • Molecular interactions and their influence on macroscopic properties
  • Engineering functional materials
  • Introduction to macromolecules
  • Nanoscale systems
  • Sustainability, circular materials, and commercialization using molecular engineering

Continuous Improvement Actions
Assessment finding Planned course response
Students need stronger connections among phase diagrams, microconstituents, and phase-rule calculations. Add more in-class examples that explicitly connect these concepts and calculations.
Students need additional support distinguishing phases from microconstituents. Increase direct instruction and guided practice on recognizing the difference.
Students need greater clarity when selecting values from phase diagrams. More clearly identify which diagram values are required for each calculation.
Course Climate feedback identified perceived overlap with CME 260 Properties of Materials. Coordinate CHE 230 with CME 260, CHE 330, and CHE 454 to reduce unnecessary repetition while preserving useful conceptual reinforcement.
CHE 230 must maintain a distinct molecular-systems role in the curriculum. Consistently connect course topics to molecular structure, bonding, microstructure, structure-property relationships, and chemical engineering applications.
