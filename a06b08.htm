ChE 230 - Molecular Systems in Chemical Engineering
UIC Course Description Introduction to fundamental concepts in molecular engineering and materials chemistry; properties of molecular systems and applications of macromolecules, bio-macromolecules and nanomaterials in energy, medicine, environment and technology. Course Information: 3 hours. Prerequisite(s): CHEM 232.
