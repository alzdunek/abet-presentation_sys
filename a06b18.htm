ChE 396 - Senior Design I
UIC Course Description Introduction to modern, process design and development, engineering economics, and report writing. Design and cost of equipment relating to materials handling to heat transfer, mass transfer, and reactors. Course Information: 3 or 4 hours. Prerequisite(s): CHE 312 and CHE 313 and Credit or concurrent registration in CHE 321.
