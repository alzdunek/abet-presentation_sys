ChE 433 - Process Simulation With Aspen Plus
UIC Course Description Application of Aspen Plus to design, modeling and simulation of process flow sheets. Property models, unit operations, heat integration and pinch analysis, electrolytes, nonconventional solids (e.g., coal), computational aspects. Course Information: 3 undergraduate hours. 4 graduate hours. Prerequisite(s): CHE 312 and CHE 313 and CHE 321; or consent of the instructor.
