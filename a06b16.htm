ChE 381 - Chemical Engineering Laboratory I
UIC Course Description Heat and momentum transfer operations associated with chemical processes. These include heat exchangers, fluid properties, and fluid flow. Technical report writing, computer calculations. Course Information: 2 hours. Prerequisite(s): CHE 312.
