Feedback-driven and/or assessment-validated program changes since the 2014 ABET accreditation cycle

  1. Addition of CHE 230 Molecular Systems in Chemical Engineering; CHE 330 Polymer Science and CHEM 346 Physical Chemistry II as a Selective Option in the curriculum.

  2. Addition of technical electives to the curriculum
    • CHE 426 Biopharmaceutical Process Development and Manufacturing
    • CHE 427 Entrepreneurship in Engineering
    • CHE 454 Molecular and Macromolecular Engineering
    • CHE 455 Nanoscale Systems in Chemical Engineering
    • CHE 457 Colloidal and Interfacial Phenomena

  3. Addition of Concentrations to the BS Major
    • Concentration in Polymers and Molecular Engineering
    • Concentration in Energy and Environment
    • Concentration in Nanotechnology
    • Concentration in Process Automation
    • Concentration in Entrepreneurship

  4. Developed a chemical engineering specific “Senior Design Showcase EXPO” to compliment the College Engineering EXPO

  5. CHE 381/382 laboratory curriculum improvements
    • Continuous distillation experiment added to compliment the batch distillation experiment
    • Sample calculation sheets for improved experimental data analysis
    • Lab manual revisions for better experimental procedure clarity

  6. Improvement to extracurricular student learning experiences
    • ChemE Connect- Student-led Department action team
    • AIChE Student Chapter Initiatives: Hosted North-Central Regional AIChE Conference, Started ChemE Cube team, increased career-oriented and social events,
    • Student-student mentorship, increased fundraising, volunteering to recruitment events and community outreach

  7. Improvement to CHE 499 Professional Development Seminar
    • o Women engineer and academic engineer seminar speakers

  8. Ongoing improvement to the safety content in the curriculum
    • ChE 397 - Senior Design II- HAZOP analysis

  9. Future Improvements to the Curriculum: New Introductory CHE course (CHE 101 Introduction to Chemical Engineering and Sustainability)
