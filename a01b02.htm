Mission Statement of the University of Illinois at Chicago
Mission Statement
https://chancellor.uic.edu/about/mission/

"UIC's mission is:

  • To provide the broadest access to the highest levels of educational, research, and clinical excellence. We share a belief that access and excellence belong together, and can be delivered at scale. Our shared commitment to access, vitality, empowerment, and creativity is our collective superpower.
  • To support our mission, UIC is guided by five strategic priorities introduced by Chancellor Miranda. These priorities reaffirm UIC’s commitment to student success; expand the research profile to position the university at the forefront of innovation and Chicago’s knowledge economy; engage with the region’s communities with a focus on educational and health equity; forge productive partnerships with businesses, nonprofits, and the government sector to create opportunities for faculty and student engagement; and elevate UIC as a destination to recruit and retain world-class faculty and staff.
  • Collectively, these priorities are advancing the university’s equity-focused mission to provide the broadest access to the highest levels of educational, research, and clinical excellence.
The current UIC mission statement has been in place since a formal update was approved by the Board of Trustees resolution dated January 19, 2017.
Mission Statement of the College of Engineering
Mission Statement published February 11th 2026
https://engineering.uic.edu/about/strategic-plan/#what-drives-us

“Through our commitment to excellence, accessible education and impactful research, we prepare curious, proficient and diverse engineers and computer scientists to lead and advance upward mobility locally and globally.”

The College of Engineering mission directly supports and operationalizes the UIC mission by advancing broad access through accessible engineering education, strengthening institutional excellence through rigorous academic and research programs, and contributing to societal and economic mobility through impactful technological innovation locally and globally.

Mission Statement of the Department of Chemical Engineering
Mission Statement prior to April 2026
https://che.uic.edu/undergraduate-programs/abet-accreditation/

"The mission of the Department of Chemical Engineering is to serve society through excellence in education, research, and public service. We provide for our students an education in chemical engineering, and we aspire to instill in them the attitudes, values, and vision that will prepare them for lifetimes of continued learning and leadership in their chosen careers. Through scholarship, the Department strives to generate new knowledge and technology for the benefit of the State of Illinois, the nation, and beyond."

New Mission Statement: Revision in progress

“The mission of the Department of Chemical Engineering is to deliver an outstanding and accessible education to a diverse student body. We prepare skilled engineers to advance both fundamental and applied research, drive innovation, and lead the development of transformative technologies that enhance economic mobility and contribute to local and global prosperity”.
