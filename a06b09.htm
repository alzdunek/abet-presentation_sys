ChE 301 - Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics
UIC Course Description Review of classical engineering thermodynamics. Multicomponent systems & multicomponent phase equilibria. Equilibrium in chemically reacting systems, heterogeneous equilibrium, Gibbs phase rule, and electrochemical processes. Course Information: 3 hours. Prerequisite(s): CHE 201 and CHE 205.
